ABOUT SONIC THE HEDGEHOG 4

Sonic the Hedgehog 4 is the upcoming fourth main film in the Sonic the Hedgehog film series by Sega and Paramount. It is set to be released on March 19, 2027.

The premise is currently under wraps, though director Jeff Fowler has confirmed the plot will take elements from the 1993 Sonic the Hedgehog game Sonic CD.

The currently confirmed characters are:

  • Sonic the Hedgehog (voiced by Ben Schwartz)
  • Amy Rose (voiced by Kristen Bell)
  • Metal Sonic
  • Doctor Ivo "Eggman" Robotnik (portrayed by Jim Carrey)
  • Tom Wachowski (portrayed by James Marsden)
  • Maddie Wachowski (portrayed by Tika Sumpter)
  • Agent Stone (portrayed by Lee Majdoub)
  • Miles "Tails" Prower (voiced by Colleen O'Shaughnessey)
  • Knuckles the Echidna (voiced by Idris Elba)
  • Shadow the Hedgehog (voiced by Keanu Reeves)
  • Unknown characters performed by Ben Kingsley (voice), Nick Offerman (voice), Matt Berry, and Richard Ayoade

Filming has completed, and Sonic 4 is currently in the post-production phase, which includes the animation process. Only the title teaser video has been released for marketing.

ABOUT THE SONIC UNIVERSE EVENT FILM

The untitled spin-off film, under the working title of "Sonic Universe Event Film", in the Sonic the Hedgehog film series by Sega and Paramount is set for release on December 22, 2028. Plot details are currently under wraps.
