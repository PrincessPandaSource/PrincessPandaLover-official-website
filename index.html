❤︎PrincessPandaLover

Hey there! I'm PrincessPandaLover, a 21-year-old Chinese (yet lives in America) auDHD (both autistic and with ADHD) female ("she/her").

While I major in computer science at college, I like creating art, specifically cartoons and illustrations in my style. I'm also interested in creative writing and game development. Not to mention I'm a big Sonic the Hedgehog fan and love drawing and writing about the fast blue hedgehog, his friends, and his world. As such, I plan to be a software developer by day, hobbyist cartoonist, illustrator, indie game developer, and Sonic fan creator at night. (I do aim for professional level for the latter.)

My general interests include video games, animals, music, and crazy fun.

Fun fact: I created the logo when I was 12. Hand-drew it in the Vector Editor on Scratch.
