I lied about publishing standalone finished art before resuming Silver Scripts. As a computer science student, I'm tackling CS practice and projects as my top priority. Like I've said before, it's hard to do another major task on the same day when you have ADHD. (In March, I spun out the Sonic the Hedgehog 4 Countdown webpage. It's cool, and you should check it out.)

Inking is always the most time-consuming part of making these comics, and I'll say that the assistant tool in Krita was really helpful for inking the windows and features on the building and keeping them consistent.

I am trying to get back to creating art as a habitual hobby, but I need to say this: I may abstain from posting on social media. The pressure to make my art top-notch, the immense gamble the algorithm brings, and constantly having to interact with other artists turns the hobby into a chore, and it makes me miserable. In the next few months, I may only post artwork and doodles on my own website, where there'll be such gallery. This may apply to Silver Scripts as well, besides probably on Archive of Our Own. Please, I need to look after my mental health.
