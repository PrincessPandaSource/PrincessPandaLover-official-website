Can't you believe there was only one comic strip in 2025?!

Yeah, I tried to push another strip in December, but it's hard to follow the schedule and do everything you need to do when you have ADHD (as well as sleep deprivation). I was pretty much only able to do one big task each day, and combine that with constant dopamine-seeking and the fact I need to study Introduction to Analysis of Algorithms (which I've heard is harder than rocks) in preparation for my last college semester.

It didn't help I had perfectionist tendencies, especially during the inking process, where I drew new lines to be more accurate than the pencils by using photo manipulation features. But, hey, at least the comic strip is now done!

As you can see here, I'm trying to get a storyline going, now that I focus on arcs based on past events instead of current. Also, I want to return to finished Sonic fanart, with actual colors and shading, so depending on how I accommodate my schedule, you may see a lack of Silver Scripts strips for a while. However, this comic is definitely not going to be end! I also want to tell stories within this AU, and you'll get to see them.
